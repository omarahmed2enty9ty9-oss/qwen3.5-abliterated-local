=== Reddit (r/LocalLLaMA) ===
Qwen3.5-9B abliterated on an 8GB 2060 SUPER — setup + numbers

Wrote up what it actually takes to fit this in 8 GB, since most of the pain is VRAM contention rather than the model.

- IQ4_XS, 5.7 GB on disk, ~6.9 GB VRAM at 16K ctx
- 8/12 on my 12-task coding suite (stock Qwen3.5-9B scores 11/12 — abliteration costs real accuracy, worth knowing)
- 100% tool calling, 100% edit validity
- q8_0 KV cache roughly halves KV VRAM vs f16 default

Gotcha that cost me an afternoon: Qwen3.5 defaults to thinking ON and will burn the whole token budget reasoning, returning an empty response. `think: false` is a top-level request field, not an options key, and `PARAMETER think false` in a Modelfile is rejected by current Ollama.

Config only, no weights. Credits to Qwen / huihui_ai / mradermacher.
